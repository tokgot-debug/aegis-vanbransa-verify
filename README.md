# AEGIS × VANBRANSA — Anti-Counterfeit Infrastructure & AI Analytics

**Project Aegis** (co-branded with **VANBRANSA Digital Out-Of-Home**) gives every beverage unit a phone-checkable cryptographic identity, binds it to the KRA excise stamp, and scores every scan, shipment, and field test in real time to detect counterfeit batches and the syndicate networks behind them.

## Core Modules
- **Overview (`#/`)**: Live `SCAN ENGINE · LIVE` scoring card (`SNT-FA24-57D2-13EE-EC26`, `ALREADY OPENED`, `RISK 74`), dynamic industrial bottling-line background, and Kenya illicit beverage market telemetry (`60%`, `Sh120bn`, `9.6m`, `1 in 5`).
- **Verify Bottle (`#/verify`)**: Optical & 365nm UV spectral scanner, cryptographic unit-to-stamp verification, and immutable supply-chain custody ledger.
- **Blueprint (`#/blueprint`)**: 3-Layer technical architecture (`L1 Cryptographic Unit Binding`, `L2 Optical & Closure State`, `L3 Syndicate Velocity Graph`) and interactive scenario simulator.
- **Trade Intelligence (`#/trade`)**: Kenya regional counterfeit hotspot radar (`Nairobi`, `Nakuru`, `Busia`, `Mombasa`, `Eldoret`) and field enforcement warrant ledger.
- **AI Reports & Analytics (`#/reports`)**: **Aegis Cortex AI Analyst** with 1-click executive forensic briefings, custom natural-language query synthesis, monthly excise revenue recovery charts (`Sh 562M` cumulative), county risk ranking matrix, and CSV/JSON/Print dossier exports.
